# TILAWA — Monetization Strategy

## Principle
**Worship stays free. Coaching is premium.** Reading the Quran, listening to recitation, and tracking a khatm will never be paywalled — both as an ethical stance and as the growth engine. Revenue comes from the AI coaching layer, convenience, and institutions. **No advertising, ever.**

## 1. Free Tier (forever)
- Full mushaf reader, all translations
- Unlimited listening (all reciters incl. Yasser Al-Dosari)
- Streaks, khatm tracker, bookmarks
- 5 AI-scored recitations per week
- Tajweed rule library (read-only)

## 2. TILAWA Premium — $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr (regional pricing)
- Unlimited AI recitation scoring + full heatmap history
- Waveform replay & progress analytics
- Tajweed drills, quizzes, personalized weak-rule plans
- Whisper Mode, sleep timer, offline audio downloads
- Battles, leaderboards, seasonal events
- Mood-Based Verse Suggester, Asbab al-Nuzul story library

Regional pricing: PPP-adjusted tiers for South/Southeast Asia (~$1.49–1.99/mo equivalent); prepaid-friendly options (Google Play carrier billing).

## 3. Family Plan — $9.99/mo
- Up to 6 members, parent dashboard, child-safe accounts, shared khatm challenges.
- Key wedge for the diaspora beachhead market.

## 4. Institutional (Madrasa/School) Licensing
- Per-seat annual licensing with teacher dashboard, assignments, cohort analytics.
- Pilot free for first semester; sold via masjid/Islamic-school partnerships.
- Second revenue engine; also a distribution channel (students convert families).

## 5. Seasonal & Community Revenue
- **Ramadan gift subscriptions** ("Gift a year of Quran learning") — highest-intent moment of the year.
- **Sadaqah sponsorships**: donors sponsor premium access for students in low-income regions (transparent counter). Revenue + mission + marketing in one mechanic.

## 6. What We Will Not Do
- Ads or data monetization of any kind.
- Paywalling Quran text, audio, or khatm tracking.
- Selling "religious merit" framing (no "pay to earn more reward" messaging).
- Dark-pattern trials; cancellation is one tap.

## 7. Unit Economics (working model)
| Metric | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Blended ARPU (paid) | ~$3.20/mo after regional mix + stores' cut |
| Marginal cost/paid user | ~$0.35/mo (ML inference dominant; mitigated by caching + quotas) |
| Target paid conversion | 4% of MAU by month 12 |
| CAC channels | Organic/creator-led first; paid UA only post-retention proof |

## 8. Sequencing
1. **Q1–Q2**: everything free (growth + scoring-data flywheel).
2. **Q3**: introduce Premium with grandfathered early-adopter pricing.
3. **Q4**: Family plan + institutional pilots; Ramadan gift campaign.
