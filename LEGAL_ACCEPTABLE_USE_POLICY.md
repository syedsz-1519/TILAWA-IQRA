# TILAWA — Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** September 2024  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Version:** 1.0 (Final)

## 📋 Introduction

The Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") defines the rules for how you may use TILAWA. By using the Service, you agree to comply with this policy. Violations may result in warnings, account suspension, or permanent termination.

---

## 🚫 Prohibited Content

You agree **NOT** to upload, create, post, or transmit content that:

### 1. Illegal or Harmful Content

- Is unlawful in your jurisdiction or internationally
- Constitutes fraud, scams, or deception
- Promotes or facilitates violence or terrorism
- Contains malware, viruses, or harmful code
- Infringes on intellectual property rights
- Violates intellectual property of others

### 2. Hateful or Discriminatory Content

- Promotes hatred based on race, ethnicity, religion, caste, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any protected characteristic
- **Specific to TILAWA**: Content disrespectful or hateful toward Islam, the Quran, the Prophet (peace be upon him), or any other faith community
- Incites violence or harassment against individuals or groups
- Contains slurs, dehumanizing language, or bigoted stereotypes

### 3. Sexually Explicit or Indecent Content

- Pornography or sexually explicit material
- Solicitation of sexual services
- Non-consensual intimate imagery (revenge porn)
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — we have **zero tolerance** and report to NCMEC/authorities

### 4. Harassment and Bullying

- Personal attacks, threats, or intimidation
- Harassment of individuals or groups
- Doxxing (sharing private information to enable harassment)
- Stalking or repeated unwanted contact
- Organized harassment campaigns

### 5. Misinformation and Disinformation

- False claims that cause significant harm (e.g., public health hoaxes)
- Deliberate spread of false religious teachings presented as authoritative
- Election interference or voter misinformation
- Impersonation of others to deceive

### 6. Privacy Violations

- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Capturing screenshots of private messages and sharing publicly
- Recording others without consent (where illegal)
- Sharing home addresses, phone numbers, financial info, etc.

### 7. Commercial Misuse

- Spam, unsolicited advertising, or MLM recruitment
- Unauthorized commercial activity
- Selling, trading, or promoting illegal substances
- Counterfeit goods or scam products

## 🔧 Prohibited Technical Activities

### 1. Infrastructure Abuse

- DDoS attacks or attempts to disrupt the Service
- Overloading servers with requests
- Exploiting bugs to gain unauthorized access
- Attempting to breach security measures

### 2. Scraping and Extraction

- Automated scraping of Quranic content, audio, or user data
- Downloading recitation audio in bulk for redistribution
- Extracting user practice data without authorization
- Bypass of access controls or authentication

### 3. Reverse Engineering

- Decompiling, disassembling, or reverse-engineering the app
- Attempting to discover security vulnerabilities
- Creating unauthorized derivatives or clones
- Circumventing copy protection or DRM

### 4. Account Abuse

- Creating multiple accounts to evade bans
- Sharing login credentials with unauthorized persons
- Trading, selling, or buying accounts
- Using bots or automated tools (without permission)
- Impersonating other users or creating fake accounts

### 5. API Misuse

- Exceeding rate limits intentionally
- Using API tokens of other users
- Scraping API endpoints
- Reverse-engineering API logic

## 🚨 Enforcement and Consequences

### 1. Warnings

First-time minor violations may result in:
- In-app warning
- Temporary feature restriction
- Opportunity to correct

### 2. Account Suspension

Repeated or serious violations may result in:
- Account suspension (7–30 days)
- Inability to participate in battles/leaderboards
- Temporary loss of premium features
- Forfeiture of ranking/streaks

### 3. Permanent Termination

Severe or repeated violations result in:
- Permanent account termination
- Data deletion (per Privacy Policy)
- Blacklisting (prevention of re-registration)
- Reporting to authorities (for illegal activity)

**Violations triggering immediate termination:**
- CSAM or sexual exploitation content
- Terrorist content or violence incitement
- Fraud or criminal activity
- Repeated targeted harassment
- DDoS or infrastructure attacks

### 4. Appeals

If you believe your account was suspended unfairly:
1. Email support@tilawa.app with **"Appeal"** in subject
2. Provide your account email and explanation
3. We review within **7 days**
4. Final decision is binding

A: We do not monitor private messages unless reported. If harassed, report the user.

A: Disagreement ≠ violation. Only harassment, hate speech, or misinformation is actionable.

**Q: What if I disagree with a moderation decision?**  
A: You can appeal. Email support@tilawa.app with **"Appeal"** in subject.

**Q: Are there exceptions for religious/cultural reasons?**  
A: Context matters. We respect Islamic and cultural norms, but illegal content is never permitted.

**Q: Can brands/organizations use TILAWA for promotion?**  
A: No commercial promotion without explicit partnership. Contact business@tilawa.app.
