## 2. What Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:
- **Email address** (required for account login and recovery)
- **Password** (hashed with bcrypt/Argon2, never stored in plain text)
- **Display name** (optional; for community features)
- **Date of birth** (to verify age requirements)
- **Subscription/billing information** (if applicable)

### 2.2 Voice Recordings

When you use the recitation or Tajweed practice features, we collect:
- **Your audio recordings** of Quranic recitation
- **Metadata**: duration, timestamp, surah/ayah, language settings
- **Processing results**: AI feedback scores, corrections, suggestions

**Classification**: 🔴 **Highly Sensitive**

**Your Control**:
- Recordings are **private by default**
- We auto-delete recordings after **90 days** (default setting)
- You can request **permanent deletion within 24 hours**
- You can disable recording entirely

**NOT used for**:
- Training AI models (unless you explicitly opt-in)
- Selling to third parties
- Advertising profiling
- Public sharing

### 2.3 Practice and Progress Data

We collect:
- **Streaks and XP points** (daily activity tracking)
- **Reading progress** (which surah/ayah you last read)
- **Bookmarks** (your saved ayahs and notes)
- **Quiz scores** (Tajweed assessments, Hadith tests)
- **Favorites** (saved Hadiths and Duas)
- **Spiritual reflections** (Nafs tracking journal entries)
- **Community participation** (battles, challenges, leaderboard rank)

**Classification**: 🟡 **Sensitive** (reveals religious practice patterns)

**Your Control**:
- Exportable as JSON anytime
- Deleted with your account
- Never shared with third parties
- Not used for advertising profiling

### 2.4 Device and Technical Data

We collect:
- **Device type** (iOS, Android, Web)
- **Operating system and app version**
- **IP address** (for abuse detection and DDoS prevention)
- **User agent string** (browser/app info)
- **Performance metrics** (crashes, load times)
- **Accessibility settings** (screen reader use, font sizes)

**Classification**: 🟢 **Low Sensitivity**

**Retention**: 13 months, then deleted

### 2.5 Usage Analytics

We collect:
- **Features used** (time spent on Tajweed, battles, etc.)
- **Pages visited**
- **Errors encountered**
- **Session duration**

**Classification**: 🟢 **Low Sensitivity**

**Method**: Privacy-respecting first-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no ad trackers)

### 2.6 Payment Information

If you subscribe:
- **Card/payment method** (processed by Stripe, Apple, Google)
- **Billing address** (for invoicing)
- **Transaction history**

**Classification**: 🟡 **Sensitive**

**Security**: PCI DSS compliant; encrypted in transit; we never store full card numbers

## 6. Security Measures

### 6.1 Authentication & Authorization

- **Password hashing**: Argon2/bcrypt (not plain text)
- **Session management**: HttpOnly, SameSite cookies (web); JWT with short TTL (mobile)
- **Rate limiting**: Brute-force protection on login (max 5 attempts/5 min)
- **Breach screening**: Check password against compromised password databases
- **Optional 2FA**: Coming in Phase 3

### 6.2 Data Protection

- **Encryption in transit**: TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS everywhere)
- **Encryption at rest**: AES-256 for sensitive data
- **Signed URLs**: For voice recording access (expiry-based)
- **Row-level security**: Database queries scoped by user_id

### 6.3 Application Security

- **Input validation**: All endpoints validate/sanitize input
- **Parameterized queries**: Prevents SQL injection
- **CSRF protection**: Token-based CSRF defense
- **XSS prevention**: Content Security Policy headers
- **API rate limiting**: Per-user quotas prevent abuse

### 6.4 Infrastructure

- **WAF**: Web Application Firewall blocks malicious traffic
- **DDoS protection**: CloudFlare or similar
- **Intrusion detection**: Alerts on suspicious activity
- **Regular audits**: Quarterly penetration testing
- **Dependency scanning**: Automated vulnerability checks

### 6.5 Access Controls

- **Principle of least privilege**: Employees access only necessary data
- **Role-based access**: Developers ≠ Admins ≠ Support
- **Audit logs**: All admin access is logged and monitored
- **No backdoors**: No master account that can bypass user-level checks
