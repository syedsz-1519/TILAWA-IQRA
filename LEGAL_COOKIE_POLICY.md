## 🍪 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites and apps remember information about you. They are essential for many online services, but TILAWA uses them **responsibly and transparently**.

---

## 📋 Cookies We Use

### 1. Essential / Necessary Cookies 🔴

**Purpose**: These cookies are **required** for the app to function. You cannot opt-out without disabling core features.

| Cookie Name | Duration | Purpose |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| `session_id` | 30 days or until logout | Keeps you logged in securely |
| `csrf_token` | Session | Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks |
| `user_preferences` | 1 year | Remembers your language, theme (light/dark), accessibility settings |
| `recitation_playback_state` | Session | Remembers last position in audio playback |

**Owner**: TILAWA  
**Opt-out**: Not recommended; may break login and core features

---

### 2. Analytics Cookies 🟡

**Purpose**: These cookies help us understand how you use the app so we can improve it.

| Cookie Name | Duration | Purpose | Data Collected |
|-------------|----------|---------|-----------------|
| `_plausible` | 1 year | Privacy-respecting analytics | Page views, session duration, features used |
| `plausible_session` | 30 minutes | Session tracking | Distinguishes visits |

**Owner**: Plausible Analytics (we use privacy-first analytics, not Google Analytics)  
**Data**: Anonymized; no personal data linked  
**Opt-out**: Available in account settings

---

### 3. Performance Cookies 🟢

**Purpose**: Help us monitor app performance and detect errors.

| Cookie Name | Duration | Purpose |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| `sentry_session` | Session | Error tracking and debugging |
| `web_vitals` | Session | Monitors page speed and performance |

**Owner**: Sentry, internal monitoring  
**Opt-out**: No impact on app functionality

## ⚙️ Cookie Management

### How to Control Cookies

**In TILAWA (Recommended)**:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Privacy & Security**
2. Toggle **"Analytics Cookies"** on/off
3. Your choice is saved immediately

**In Your Browser**:
1. **Chrome**: Settings → Privacy and security → Delete cookies
2. **Safari**: Preferences → Privacy → Manage website data
3. **Firefox**: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
4. **Edge**: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data

### Disable All Cookies

You can disable cookies entirely in browser settings, but this may:
- Break login functionality
- Disable saved preferences
- Affect playback resume

## 🛡️ Cookie Security

**Our Practices**:
- ✅ HttpOnly flag: Cookies inaccessible to JavaScript (prevents XSS theft)
- ✅ Secure flag: Cookies sent only over HTTPS (no plain HTTP)
- ✅ SameSite=Strict: Prevents CSRF attacks
- ✅ Expiration dates: Cookies expire automatically
- ✅ Encryption: Sensitive cookies are encrypted

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I use TILAWA without cookies?**  
A: Essential cookies are required for login. You can disable analytics and performance cookies without affecting core features.

**Q: How long are cookies stored?**  
A: Typically 30 days to 1 year. Session cookies expire when you close the app/browser.

**Q: Can I delete cookies anytime?**  
A: Yes, anytime through browser settings or app settings. You may need to log in again.

**Q: Do you track me across websites?**  
A: No. We only track your activity within TILAWA. We do not use retargeting or cross-site cookies.

**Q: What about mobile apps?**  
A: Mobile apps use local storage instead of "cookies" but follow the same privacy principles.

**Q: Can I opt-out of all cookies?**  
A: You can opt-out of analytics/performance cookies. Essential cookies cannot be disabled (app won't work otherwise).

**Q: Do you sell cookie data?**  
A: No. We do not sell any data, including cookie data.
