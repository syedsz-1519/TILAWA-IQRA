# TILAWA — Strategic Market Analysis

## 1. Market Size

- ~2 billion Muslims worldwide; ~1.2 billion smartphone users among them.
- Islamic app economy: Quran, prayer, and lifestyle apps collectively exceed **500M+ installs**; Ramadan drives 30–150% seasonal usage spikes.
- Online Quran tutoring is a fragmented, multi-hundred-million-dollar services market (typically $8–20/hour) — TILAWA's AI feedback attacks this cost structure directly.
- **SAM**: Muslims actively seeking recitation improvement — conservatively 100M+ (madrasa students, new practitioners, converts, diaspora parents).
- **SOM (3 yr)**: 5M MAU across English-first diaspora markets (US, UK, EU), Gulf, South/Southeast Asia.

## 2. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Strength | Gap TILAWA exploits |
|---|---|---|
| **Quran.com / Quran app** | Canonical text, huge SEO, free | No feedback loop, no learning system |
| **Muslim Pro** | Massive installs, prayer utility | Ads-heavy, shallow Quran learning; past privacy controversies |
| **Tarteel AI** | AI recitation follow-along, memorization mistakes | Focused on *memorization detection*; tajweed-quality scoring and gamified learning are underserved |
| **Quran Companion / Quranly** | Habit/streak mechanics | No AI, no recitation feedback |
| **Bayyinah / traditional academies** | Deep scholarship, trust | Video courses, not interactive; expensive |
| **Human tutor platforms (e.g. Qutor-style)** | Real teacher quality | $10–20/hr, scheduling friction, supply-constrained |

**Positioning**: *Tarteel checks what you say; TILAWA teaches how you say it.* The tajweed-quality feedback loop + habit system + reverent design is an open lane.

## 3. Differentiation (defensible)
1. **Tajweed scoring engine** — expert-labeled dataset compounds over time (data moat).
2. **Whisper Mode** — unique low-volume model for the real practice context (late night).
3. **Battles + constellation progression** — engagement mechanics designed for worship, not against it.
4. **Scholar governance** — public review-board model builds trust competitors can't fake quickly.

## 4. Go-To-Market

### Beachhead: English-speaking diaspora (US/UK/CA/AU)
- Highest willingness to pay, most acute teacher scarcity, app-store literate.
- Channels: masjid partnerships, Islamic school pilots, Muslim YouTube/TikTok creators (recitation challenge format), Ramadan campaign.

### Expansion sequence
1. Gulf (Arabic UI, prestige reciters) →
2. South Asia (Urdu; low-end Android optimization; prepaid-friendly pricing) →
3. Southeast Asia (Indonesian/Malay; largest single market).

### Seasonal strategy
- **Ramadan is the Super Bowl**: 6-week pre-Ramadan feature freeze + campaign; khatm challenges; gift subscriptions.

## 5. Business Model
See `monetization.md` for detail. Summary: freemium — listening + basic reader free forever; AI scoring quota, battles, advanced analytics, and family plans in premium ($4.99/mo, regional pricing). Institutional licensing for madrasas as second engine. **No ads, ever** (trust-critical).

## 6. Risks & Headwinds

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Tarteel expands into tajweed scoring | High | Speed + engagement layer + governance trust; they are memorization-anchored |
| Religious skepticism of "AI judging Quran" | High | Frame as *practice aid, not authority*; visible scholar review board; imam endorsements |
| Free alternatives (piety of free Quran content) | Medium | Keep worship-core free; charge only for AI coaching |
| Privacy backlash (voice data, Muslim user data sensitivity) | High | No ads/trackers, on-device options, deletion SLA, transparent policy |
| Low-end device performance in key markets | Medium | Lite mode, aggressive audio caching, small APK |

## 7. Why Now
- Whisper-class ASR made Quranic-Arabic recognition viable at consumer cost only recently.
- Post-COVID normalization of remote religious learning.
- Gen-Z Muslim identity resurgence; demand for high-craft Islamic apps outstrips supply of well-designed options.
